---
name: post-instagram-story
description: Publish a story (one picture or video) from the Instagram app.
---
Publish a new Instagram story with the {{kind}} that was just put on this phone, from the Instagram app. It
is the newest item in the gallery (top left in the picker); use exactly that one. A story has no caption:
its words are already on the {{kind}}, so add no text, stickers or music.

1. Open the Instagram app (package com.instagram.android). If it is not installed, stop and say so. If it
   shows a login screen, stop and say the account must be logged in first; never type a password.
2. Close anything in the way (notification prompts, "Not now" dialogs) with "Not now", "Cancel" or the X.
3. Tap your own profile picture with the "+" at the top left of the home feed ("Your story"), or tap "+"
   (Create) and choose STORY at the bottom.
4. If a camera opens, tap the gallery thumbnail at the bottom left. Tap the first (top left) item.
5. In the story editor change nothing. Tap "Your story" (bottom left) or the arrow "->" then "Share" next to
   "Your story". Do not choose "Close friends".
6. Wait until the story has finished uploading (your profile picture at the top left of the feed gets a
   coloured ring, or the upload indicator disappears), and stop on that screen: your last screenshot is kept
   as the proof that it is up.

Report in one or two sentences whether the story is now published, or exactly where it stopped and what the
screen said.
