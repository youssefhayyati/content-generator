name: post-instagram-feed
description: Publish a feed post (one picture, or a carousel of several) from the Instagram app.
---
Publish a new Instagram feed post with {{media}} and the caption below, from the Instagram app on this phone.

The {{media}} for it were just put on the phone. In the gallery they are the {{count}} newest items, already
in the right order: the newest (top left in the picker) is the first slide, the next one is the second, and so
on. Use exactly those, nothing older.

1. The Instagram app was just closed and opened anew, so it should show its home feed; if it isn't open, open
   it (package com.instagram.android). If it is not installed, stop and say so. If it shows a login screen,
   stop and say the account must be logged in first; never type a password. If it offers to continue an
   earlier draft or unfinished post, don't: start a new one ("Start new", "Discard" or "Not now"), even if
   that drops the old draft. It is never the post asked for here.
2. Close anything in the way (a "Turn on notifications" prompt, a "Not now" dialog, a story or ad) with
   "Not now", "Cancel" or the X.
3. Start a new post: tap the "+" (Create) button at the top left of the Home feed (the middle of the bottom bar
   is Messages, not Create). If a choice of POST / STORY / REEL appears at the bottom, make sure POST is selected.
4. Pick the media in the gallery (the "Recents" grid):
   - One item: tap the first (top left) thumbnail.
   - More than one: first turn on multiple selection (the "Select multiple" button or the stacked squares
     icon above the grid), then tap the first {{count}} thumbnails in order, left to right, top row first,
     so they are numbered 1 to {{count}}.
   If the big preview crops the picture to a square, tap the small two-arrows icon at the bottom left of the
   preview so the whole picture (portrait 4:5) shows.
5. Tap "Next" (top right, or the arrow). On the filters / edit screen change nothing; if it asks to add
   music, skip it. Tap "Next" again.
6. On the screen with "Write a caption..." / "Add a caption...", tap that box and type the caption exactly
   as given below, the way it says (a long one in pieces, one type action each). Never press enter. If a list
   of hashtag or name suggestions pops up while typing, ignore it and keep typing. Do not add, fix or translate
   anything.

Caption:
{{caption}}

7. Before sharing, look at the caption box: if words are missing or doubled, clear the box and type it again.
   Close the keyboard with back if it covers the button, but do not leave the screen.
8. Tap "Share" (the blue button at the bottom, or the check mark top right). If a pop-up about sharing
   (reels, Facebook, audience) appears, choose the option that just shares this post ("Share", "Not now").
9. Wait until the home feed shows the post being uploaded and then finished ("Posting..." then gone,
   "Posted", or your new post at the top). If it says "Couldn't post" or "Try again", tap retry once.
10. Stop on a screen that shows the new post (the feed with it at the top, or "Posted"): your last screenshot
   is kept as the proof that it is up. Only if you can't tell, open your profile and look at the newest post.

Report in one or two sentences whether the post is now published, or exactly where it stopped and what the
screen said.
