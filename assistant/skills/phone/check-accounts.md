---
name: check-accounts
description: Check that the Instagram and X apps are installed and logged in, and which accounts they use.
---
Check whether this phone is ready to post on Instagram and X. Only look: do not install, log in, post, follow
or change anything, and never type a password.

1. Open the Instagram app (package com.instagram.android). Note whether it is installed, whether it opens on
   a login screen, and if it is logged in, the account's username (on the profile, bottom right).
2. Press home. Open the X app (package com.twitter.android). Note the same: installed, logged in, and the
   @handle (tap the profile picture at the top left).
3. Press home.

Report in two short sentences, one per app: installed or not, logged in or not, and which account.
