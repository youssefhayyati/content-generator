description: Publish a reel (one video) from the Instagram app.
---
Publish a new Instagram reel with the video that was just put on this phone ({{media}}) and the caption below,
from the Instagram app. The video is the newest item in the gallery (the first video in the picker); use exactly
that one.

1. The Instagram app was just closed and opened anew, so it should show its home feed; if it isn't open, open
   it (package com.instagram.android). If it is not installed, stop and say so. If it shows a login screen,
   stop and say the account must be logged in first; never type a password. If it offers to continue an
   earlier draft or unfinished post, don't: start a new one ("Start new", "Discard" or "Not now"), even if
   that drops the old draft. It is never the post asked for here.
2. Close anything in the way (notification prompts, "Not now" dialogs) with "Not now", "Cancel" or the X.
3. Tap the "+" (Create) button at the top left of the Home feed (the middle of the bottom bar is Messages, not
   Create) and choose REEL in the choice at the bottom of the screen.
4. In the gallery, tap the newest video: the first video tile, top left (skip a camera or templates tile; its
   length is given above). If it opens a camera, tap the gallery thumbnail at the bottom left first.
5. Tap "Next" (or "Add" then "Next") until you reach the screen with "Write a caption...". Change nothing in
   the editor: no music, no text, no trimming (the video already has its music and words). If asked about
   adding music or templates, skip it.
6. Tap the caption box and type the caption exactly as given, one line at a time: type line 1, press enter,
   type line 2, and so on, doing as many lines as you can in one step. For an "(empty line)" just press
   enter. Do not press enter after the last line. Ignore hashtag suggestions; do not add, fix or translate
   anything.

Caption:
{{caption}}

7. Check the caption box: if words are missing or doubled, clear it and type it again.
8. Tap "Share". If a pop-up about sharing to Facebook or reel settings appears, pick the option that just
   shares the reel ("Share", "Not now").
9. Wait until the upload has finished (the progress bar at the top of the feed disappears or says posted).
   If it says it couldn't post, tap retry once.
10. Stop on a screen that shows the reel is up (the feed with "Posted" or the new reel): your last screenshot
   is kept as the proof. Only if you can't tell, open your profile's Reels tab and look at the newest one.

Report in one or two sentences whether the reel is now published, or exactly where it stopped and what the
screen said.
